# Terms of Sale

**Effective Date: October 1, 2026**

These Terms of Sale govern every purchase from **Sentrello LLC** ("Sentrello",
"we", "us"). They sit beside the [Terms of Service](/legal/service-terms/) and
the [Software License Agreement](/legal/sla/), and you accept all three at
checkout.

---

## 1. What is free, and what is sold

**Sentrello Core is free.** It is published under the AGPLv3, and you may run it
on as many servers as you like, for as long as you like, without paying us
anything or telling us you exist.

**What is sold** is a subscription:

| | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Sentrello Pro | $50 | $550 |
| A module | $15 – $35, most at $25 | 11 × the monthly price |
| A plugin | $15 | $165 |

Every price is in United States dollars, per instance, and **not per user**.
There is no user limit and no per-seat charge: put as many people on the
instance as the business has. Modules and plugins require Pro; a plugin also
requires the module it extends.

Prices shown on `sentrello.com` at the moment you buy are the prices that apply.

## 2. Founder pricing

Anybody who starts a subscription before **23:59 UTC on 31 December 2026** pays
**40% less, for as long as that subscription stays active**. It is applied at
checkout as a discount that does not expire, and it is not a trial rate.

Two things to know. It rides the subscription, not the account: if you cancel
and later start a new one, the new one is at the price list of that day. And it
carries onto modules you add to that same subscription later.

## 3. Payment, tax and invoices

Payments are taken by **Stripe**. We never see your full card number.

Tax is calculated by Stripe at checkout from the address and tax identifiers you
give, and shown before you pay. US sales tax, Canadian GST/HST and provincial
taxes, UK VAT and EU VAT are handled that way; if you are a business with a
valid VAT or GST number, enter it at checkout and the reverse charge applies
where the law allows it. It is your responsibility to give an accurate address
and tax number, and to account for tax in your own jurisdiction.

Your invoices are in the account area at any time.

## 4. Renewals

**Subscriptions renew automatically** — monthly on the same day each month, or
annually on the same date each year — at the then-current price for your plan,
less any founder discount you hold. We charge the payment method on file.

We will email you before an annual renewal. A monthly renewal is charged without
a reminder, which is what "monthly" means.

You can cancel at any time in the account area, and canceling stops the next
renewal.

---

## 5. Canceling, and what happens to the software

Cancel and you keep what you have paid for: **access runs to the end of the
period already paid**, and there is no pro-rata refund of the unused part.

After that, the instance falls back to Sentrello Core. Paid features stop being
offered and **no data is deleted** — the records those features created stay in
your database, on your server, and come back if you subscribe again.

If a payment fails rather than being cancelled, you get **14 days** in which
everything keeps working while the card is fixed. The product says so on every
screen, with the date. After 14 days the instance falls back to Core in the same
way.

## 6. Refunds

**All fees are non-refundable.** This is not the conventional software refund
policy and it is deliberate.

There is no refund for:

- changing your mind, at any point, including the first day;
- a module or plugin bought by mistake, or bought and not set up;
- unused time on a subscription you cancel;
- an instance you never installed, or installed and did not like;
- downtime, on your server or on our license service;
- a fault in the Software, a bug, a missing feature, or a feature that works
  differently from the way you expected;
- the Software not suiting your business, your accountant, your jurisdiction or
  your hardware.

**We will refund two things:** a charge that was duplicated, which we can see in
Stripe as plainly as you can; and anything applicable law requires us to refund
and does not let us contract out of.

Why it is written this way: the Software runs on your infrastructure, we cannot
take it back, and Sentrello Core is free — so you can run the product, read
every line of the paid tier's documentation, and see the whole of what you are
buying before you pay for any of it. If you are unsure, install Core first.

Before you buy, you can: read [every module's
features](/modules/), read [the published documentation](https://docs.sentrello.com),
and run the Free tier for as long as you like.

## 7. Changes to prices

We may change the price list. A change never affects a period you have already
paid for, and for an existing subscription it takes effect at the renewal after
we tell you — at least 30 days' notice by email for an annual subscription, and
at least one billing period for a monthly one. If you do not want the new price,
cancel before that renewal.

A founder discount survives a price change.

## 8. Chargebacks

Talk to us first — **support@sentrello.com** — because most disputes are a
duplicate charge or a card somebody else in the business used, and both are
fixed in minutes.

A chargeback raised without contacting us suspends the license key while it is
being investigated, and the fees charged by the payment provider are added to
what is owed if the dispute is decided in our favor.
