# CCPA

**Effective Date: October 1, 2026**

Read [the privacy policy](/legal/privacy/) first. This page adds what the
California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, asks a business to
state specifically. It applies to California residents, and the word "personal
information" is used with its CCPA meaning.

**Business:** Sentrello LLC, Colorado, United States.
**Contact:** privacy@sentrello.com

---

## 1. We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for
cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done either in the preceding
twelve months, and we have no plans to. There is no "Do Not Sell or Share My
Personal Information" toggle here because there is nothing to switch off.

We also do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose
other than the ones the CCPA permits without a right to limit — and, as far as
we can tell, we do not collect any.

---

## 2. Categories we collect, and what we do with them

| CCPA category | What, specifically | Where it comes from | Why | Who we disclose it to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identifiers | Name, email address, IP address, license key, instance identifier | You, and your instance | Run the account, verify the license, answer support | DigitalOcean, Stripe, Resend, Cloudflare |
| Commercial information | Subscriptions bought, invoices, payment history | You, and Stripe | Take payment, keep tax records | Stripe |
| Internet activity | Pages viewed on sentrello.com, referrer, user agent | Your browser, with consent for analytics | Understand which pages help | Google (analytics only, with consent) |
| Geolocation (coarse) | Country or region inferred from IP | Cloudflare | Tax calculation, and deciding whether to ask for analytics consent | Cloudflare, Stripe |
| Professional information | Company name, role if you tell us | You | Support and account management | — |

We do not collect: biometric information, precise geolocation, government
identifiers, health information, race or ethnicity, religious belief, union
membership, sexual orientation, or the contents of your mail, email or text
messages.

**Sources** are you, your browser, your instance's license check, and our
payment processor. **Purposes** are the ones listed in the table, and nothing
else.

---

## 3. What is not here, and why that is the point

The business records inside your Sentrello instance — your customers, their
addresses, what they bought, what they owe — are **not** collected by us, not
disclosed by us, and not retained by us. They are on your server. Under the
CCPA, that data makes **you** the business and us neither a service provider nor
a third party in respect of it, because we do not receive it.

If you are a California business using Sentrello to hold your own customers'
information, your obligations to those people are yours. The product is built to
help you meet them: one subject access answer across every module, erasure that
reaches all of them, and an opt-out recorded against the contact with the date
it was received and carried into every export.

## 4. Your rights

As a California resident you may:

- **Know** what personal information we have collected about you, the categories
  of sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients;
- **Access** a copy of it, in a portable form;
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information;
- **Delete** it, subject to the exceptions the statute allows;
- **Opt out** of sale or sharing — not applicable, since we do neither;
- **Limit** the use of sensitive personal information — not applicable, since we
  collect none;
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising any of these rights. We will
  not change your price, your support or your product because you asked.

**How:** email **privacy@sentrello.com**. We confirm receipt within 10 business
days and answer within **45 days**, extending once by a further 45 days if it is
genuinely complicated, and telling you if we do.

**Verification:** we will match your email address to your account. If you have
no account with us and we hold nothing under your email address, we will tell
you that rather than ask for identity documents we have no business holding.

**An authorized agent** may act for you with written permission, and we will ask
you to confirm it directly.

The exception worth naming: we cannot delete an invoice that tax law requires us
to keep, and we will say so rather than quietly ignoring the request.

## 6. Notice at collection

This page, together with [the privacy policy](/legal/privacy/), is the notice at
collection required by section 1798.100. It is linked from every page of
sentrello.com, and from the consent banner shown to California visitors before
any analytics cookie is set.

## 7. Other states

If you live in Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana or
another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have broadly the rights in
section 4, and we handle your request the same way. Write to the same address.
We do not sell personal information in any state, and we run no targeted
advertising anywhere.
