# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

Sentrello LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you visit `sentrello.com`, create an account, purchase subscriptions, or interact with our self-hosted applications (Sentrello Core and Sentrello Pro).

---

## 1. Data We Collect

### A. Account & Billing Information (sentrello.com)
When you create an account or purchase a commercial subscription on `sentrello.com`, we collect:
* **Account Details:** Full Name, Email Address.
* **Billing Details:** Billing Address, Payment Details (processed securely via Stripe; Sentrello LLC does not directly store full credit card numbers).
* **Network Identifiers:** IP Address, browser type, and device information.

### B. Open-Source Application Telemetry (Sentrello Core)
Sentrello Core does not automatically transmit telemetry data. Operational metrics and error reporting are strictly **opt-in** during the initial installation setup. If you choose to opt-in, non-sensitive usage metrics are transmitted to assist us in improving application performance.

### C. License Verification Telemetry (Sentrello Pro)
To validate active commercial licenses, instances running `Sentrello Pro` perform a daily automated check ("phone home") to `sentrello.com`.
* **Transmitted Data:** This system request transmits **only** license key validation data.
* **Private Data Handling:** Your self-hosted application data, user logs, internal business data, and system content are **never** transmitted, viewed, or stored on Sentrello LLC servers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use collected personal and technical data for the following purposes:
* Fulfilling and managing software subscriptions, renewals, and licensing key verifications.
* Sending transactional emails, system updates, and security alerts via Resend.
* Analyzing website traffic and performance on `sentrello.com` using Google Analytics.
* Complying with tax, accounting, and legal obligations.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers
We share necessary data with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate our business and deliver commercial services:
* **Stripe:** Payment processing, merchant-of-record services, and tax collection.
* **Resend:** Transactional email delivery and customer notification.
* **Google Analytics:** Website performance and user behavior analytics on `sentrello.com`.

---

## 4. International Data Transfers & Compliance

### GDPR (European Economic Area / UK Users)
If you reside in the EEA or UK, our legal basis for processing your data includes performance of a contract (delivering product licenses), compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate commercial interests. You possess the right to:
* Access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data.
* Restrict or object to processing.
* Request data portability.

### CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)
California residents have specific rights regarding personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):
* **Right to Know:** Request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
* **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information held by us.
* **Right to Opt-Out / Non-Discrimination:** We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a request to our support desk via sentrello.com.
