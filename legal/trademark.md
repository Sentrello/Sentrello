# Trademark Guidelines

**Effective Date: October 1, 2026**

**Sentrello LLC** ("Sentrello", "we", "us") owns the Sentrello name and marks.
Sentrello Core is published under the AGPLv3, and an open-source license grants
copyright permissions — **it grants no trademark rights whatsoever**. This page
is the permission, and the limit of it.

If you are a customer using Sentrello to run your business, none of this
restricts you. It is about naming, branding and distribution.

---

## 1. The marks

This page covers every trademark, service mark, trade name, logo, wordmark and
distinctive brand asset of Sentrello LLC, registered or not, including:

- **Sentrello**
- **Sentrello Core**, **Sentrello Pro**
- **Sentrello SEO Cloud**
- **Sentrello Business Partner** and the partner mark
- the Sentrello logo and wordmark, and any future logo or slogan

---

## 2. What you may do without asking

- **Talk about us.** Refer to Sentrello by name in an article, a review, a
  podcast, a comparison, a conference talk, documentation or a forum post, as
  long as what you say is true and does not suggest we endorsed it.
- **Say what you work with.** "Built for Sentrello", "compatible with Sentrello
  Core", "we implement Sentrello" — provided it is clear that your product or
  firm is independent, and provided the name is not the most prominent thing on
  the page.
- **Compare.** Name us in a fair comparison with your own product, using facts
  you can support.
- **Link to us.** Use the wordmark or logo as a link to `sentrello.com`, without
  altering it.

Use the name as an adjective, never as your own noun: "a Sentrello
implementation", not "a Sentrello".

---

## 3. Forks and modified versions: rename them

The AGPLv3 lets you fork Sentrello Core, modify it and redistribute it. The
trademark does not travel with that permission.

If you fork, modify or redistribute the `Sentrello/Sentrello` repository — or
build your own packages, binaries or container images from it — you **must**:

1. **Choose a different name.** It may not contain "Sentrello" or anything
   confusingly similar.
2. **Remove our branding** from the source, the interface, the documentation and
   the metadata before you distribute it: logos, wordmarks, the favicon, the
   product name in page titles and emails.
3. **Say where it came from, factually.** "A fork of the AGPLv3 code originally
   published by Sentrello LLC" is fine and welcome. "Sentrello Community
   Edition" is not.

The `LICENSE` file's module linking exception lets you write and sell a module
for Sentrello. It gives you no right to put our name on it beyond the compatible-
with wording in section 2.

## 4. What needs our written permission

Unless we have agreed it in writing, you may not:

- **Register or use a domain, subdomain, social handle or account name
  containing "Sentrello"** — `sentrello-hosting.com`, `@SentrelloSupport`,
  `sentrello.io`;
- **Publish a package, image or module named with "Sentrello"** to any registry:
  npm, Docker Hub, PyPI, GitHub Packages, a Linux distribution;
- **Sell hosting, managed service, support or add-ons in a way that suggests we
  authorized it** — including advertising as "official", "certified" or
  "authorized" without being a partner at a tier that grants it;
- **Use our logo in your own logo**, on merchandise, or altered in shape, color
  or proportion;
- **Bid on our marks in paid search** in a way that presents your offering as
  ours;
- **Use a name, logo or visual style confusingly similar to ours.**

Ask: **support@sentrello.com**, with "Trademark" in the subject. We answer
within two business days, and the answer is often yes.

## 5. Partners

A firm with a current [Partner Agreement](/legal/partners/) may use the
**Sentrello Business Partner** mark for as long as the partnership is live,
subject to three rules:

1. Use the mark we supply, unaltered, at the tier you currently hold.
2. Keep your own name the most prominent thing on the page. You are an
   independent business; "Sentrello Business Partner" describes a relationship,
   not who you are.
3. Stop using it when the partnership ends, including in print, on stationery
   and in email signatures, within 30 days.

You may say you implement, configure, support and train on Sentrello. You may not
say you are Sentrello, that you are its author, or that you speak for it.

---

## 6. If somebody misuses the marks

Tell us: **support@sentrello.com**. We would rather write one friendly letter
early than a legal one later, and most misuse is somebody who did not think to
ask.

We reserve every right in our marks, and asking for a correction once does not
waive the right to insist on it later.
