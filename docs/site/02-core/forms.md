# Forms

Build a form, put it on your website, and have what people send arrive as a
contact or an enquiry rather than as an email you have to retype.

Forms live inside the CRM — **CRM → Forms** in the menu. That is where what
they collect ends up, so it is where they are configured.

## Building one

Add fields — text, email, phone, number, web address, choice, date and longer
text — and mark which are required. A **choice** field is a dropdown you give
the answers to; a **date** field uses the visitor's own device picker, so it
works the way they already expect on a phone.

Each form has its own settings for what happens on submission: where the person
is sent afterwards, and who is notified.

## Putting it on your site

Each form gives you a snippet to paste into your website. It works on any site,
including ones that are nothing to do with Sentrello.

## What arrives

A submission can create a contact, or attach to one that already exists if the
email address matches — so somebody who fills in two forms is one person, not
two records that share an address.

Submissions are listed with what was sent and when, and export as a
spreadsheet. The columns come from the form's own questions rather than from
whatever the first submission happened to answer — so a question added last
week is still a column, and answers to a question you have since deleted are
still in the file.

## Spam

Forms carry basic protections against automated submission. A form that is
being abused can be paused without deleting it, so the address stays valid and
the submissions you already have are not lost.
