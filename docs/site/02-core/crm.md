# CRM

Who your customers are, and what is currently happening with each of them.

## Contacts and companies

A **contact** is a person. A **company** is an organisation, and contacts
belong to it. A sole trader is just a contact with no company; nothing forces
you to invent one.

Both carry the details you would expect — addresses, phone numbers, email — and
both carry **tags**, which are the fastest way to find a group later.

Every list has a search box, and it reads more than the name. Searching contacts
also reads their email, phone, job title and **background** — the free-text
field where "met at the trade show, knows Priya" ends up, which is often the
only thing anybody can remember about a person they are trying to find again.
Searching companies reads the sector, city, website and description.

## Deals

A **deal** is a piece of work you might win, moving through stages you define.
The board shows the pipeline, the value at each stage, and what has been sitting
untouched for too long.

**A deal can become a quote**, carrying the customer, the description and the
figure — the three things somebody would otherwise retype, and three chances to
send a price that does not match what was discussed. You land on Quotes with
the new draft at the top, ready to send.

It arrives as one line, from the deal's own name and value. Sentrello does not
guess at a breakdown the deal never held.

The button is there at any stage, not only once a deal is won: quoting is often
how a deal gets won.

A quote can then become an invoice without re-entering anything — including
when the customer accepts it themselves from the portal, which raises the
invoice at the moment they say yes.

## Notes, activities and tasks

Against any contact or company you can record a **note** (something that
happened), an **activity** (a call, a meeting, an email) and a **task**
(something to do, with a date). Overdue tasks show on the dashboard.

## Importing

Bring in a spreadsheet from **Contacts → Import**. Map the columns to fields,
see how the first rows will read, and only then import. Rows that cannot be
read are listed rather than silently skipped.

## The customer portal

A contact can be given portal access, letting them sign in to see their own
quotes and invoices and pay them. They see only their own records, through the
same permission system as everybody else.
