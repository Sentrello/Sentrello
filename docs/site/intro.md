# Introduction

Sentrello is a **unified business management platform** for small businesses.
Customers, quotes, invoices, bookkeeping, bookings, files and staff accounts,
in one application, on a server you control.

Under twenty people is the size it is built for. That is the size at which five
subscriptions cost more than the work they save, and nobody has the afternoon
it takes to make five systems agree with each other.

## What makes it different

**You host it.** Sentrello runs on your own server. Your customer records, your
invoices and your books sit on a machine you control, and they stay readable
whether or not you keep paying us.

**No per-user pricing.** Your fifth member of staff costs nothing to add.
Pricing is per instance, so hiring does not move the bill.

**The free tier is a real product.** Contacts, companies, deals, invoicing,
double-entry bookkeeping, forms and user accounts are free and open source
under the AGPL. Not a trial, and nothing in it expires.

## How it fits together

Sentrello is made of **modules**. Each is an application in its own right; each
knows about the others.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core** | Contacts, invoicing, bookkeeping, forms, users. Free. |
| **Pro** | Deeper versions of the same: recurring invoices, bills, banking, budgets, multi-currency, tax. |
| **Modules** | Optional applications — Booking, Documents, Shop, Newsletter, Docs. |

A quote becomes an invoice. The invoice posts to the ledger. A payment against
it reconciles on the bank screen, and a booking made this morning sits against
a contact you already had. That joining-up is the point. It is the work
separate tools hand back to you.

## What it costs

Free stays free. Pro and the optional modules are paid and licensed per
instance, and they switch on inside the application you already have: no second
installation, nothing to migrate.

:::tip[Try it before installing anything]
The live demo already holds a small firm's worth of data, so you can click
through a real business rather than an empty screen.
:::
