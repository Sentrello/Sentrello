# Introduction

Sentrello is a **unified business management platform** for small businesses.
Customers, quotes, invoices, bookkeeping, bookings, files and staff accounts,
in one application, on a server you control.

It is aimed at businesses of fewer than twenty people — the size where five
separate subscriptions cost more than the work they save, and where nobody has
time to keep five systems agreeing with each other.

## What makes it different

**You host it.** Sentrello runs on your own server. Your customer records, your
invoices and your books stay on a machine you control, and they remain readable
whether or not you keep paying us.

**No per-user pricing.** Adding your fifth member of staff costs nothing.
Pricing is per instance, not per seat, so the software does not get more
expensive as you hire.

**The free tier is a real product.** Contacts, companies, deals, invoicing,
double-entry bookkeeping, forms and user accounts are free and open source
under the AGPL. Not a trial, not crippled, not time limited.

## How it fits together

Sentrello is made of **modules**. Each one is a working application in its own
right, and each one knows about the others.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core** | Contacts, invoicing, bookkeeping, forms, users. Free. |
| **Pro** | Deeper versions of the same: recurring invoices, bills, banking, budgets, multi-currency, tax. |
| **Modules** | Optional applications — Booking, Documents, Shop, Newsletter, Docs. |

A quote becomes an invoice; the invoice posts to the ledger; a payment against
it reconciles on the bank screen. A booking creates an appointment against a
contact you already have. That joining-up is the point — it is the work that
separate tools push back onto you.

## What it costs

The free tier is free forever. Pro and the optional modules are paid, licensed
per instance, and unlock inside the application you already have — there is no
second installation and no migration.

:::tip[Try it before installing anything]
There is a live demo with a small firm's worth of data already in it, so you
can click through a real business rather than an empty screen.
:::
